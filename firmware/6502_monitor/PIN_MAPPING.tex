\section{Hardware Safety \& Boot Sequence}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Atari RESET (GPIO 12):} Held LOW immediately on power-up to keep Atari paused.
    \item \textbf{ESP32 Boot (GPIO 0):} Safe boot via Atari's internal pull-up.
\end{itemize}

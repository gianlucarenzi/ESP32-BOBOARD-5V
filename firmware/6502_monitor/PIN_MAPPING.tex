\section{Notes}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{A0--A7 only:} the page (\$D1 or \$D5) comes from the select line.
    \item \textbf{D0--D7} are driven only to answer a read of the RAM page or of a register.
    \item \textbf{One latch at a time:} \$80 to \$D1FF: registers \$D1xx, RAM \$D5xx; \$80 to \$D5FF: registers \$D5xx, RAM \$D100--\$D1FE. Count \$xxFB--FC, bank \$xxFD--FE, latch \$xxFF.
    \item U4 channel 8 (pins 9 / 12) and J3 pins 24--25 are unconnected.
    \item All signals pass through \textbf{TXS0108E} bidirectional level shifters (3.3\,V\,$\leftrightarrow$\,5\,V).
\end{itemize}

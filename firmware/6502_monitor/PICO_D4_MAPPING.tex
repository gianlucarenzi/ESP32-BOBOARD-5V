\section{Overview}
This document provides the hardware-level mapping for the \textbf{ESP32-PICO-D4} SiP. Unlike standard modules, physical pin numbers refer to the \textbf{QFN48} package pins.

\section{Critical Design Notes}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Forbidden Pins:} Do \textbf{NOT} connect pins 19, 23, 15, 14, 28, and 1. They are internal to the SiP.
    \item \textbf{Strapping Pin 18 (GPIO 12):} Must be LOW during power-on for 3.3V Flash selection.
    \item \textbf{Thermal Pad:} Pin 49 (EPAD) must be soldered to a solid Ground plane for cooling.
    \item \textbf{Boot (GPIO 0):} Pin 25 must be HIGH for normal boot.
\end{itemize}
